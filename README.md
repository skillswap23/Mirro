# Mirro

### Same-day beauty & wellness, without the full price.

🌐 **Live product:** https://www.themirro.com

Mirro is a marketplace designed to connect customers with last-minute beauty and wellness appointments at discounted prices.

Beauty providers often have unused appointment slots caused by cancellations or gaps in their schedules. At the same time, customers may be looking for an appointment on short notice and are willing to trade flexibility for a better price.

Mirro brings these two sides together.

---

## The Problem

Beauty and wellness businesses can lose revenue when appointment slots go unfilled, particularly because of cancellations and last-minute availability.

Customers, meanwhile, may be looking for an appointment on short notice but have difficulty discovering relevant availability.

Mirro is designed to solve both sides of this problem:

* **Customers** can discover last-minute beauty and wellness availability.
* **Providers** can make otherwise unused appointment capacity available.
* **The marketplace** creates a connection between immediate demand and unused supply.

---

## The Product

Mirro makes last-minute beauty availability discoverable.

Customers can browse available services and find opportunities that fit their needs, while providers can make available appointment slots visible to potential customers.

The core concept is:

> **Turn empty appointment slots into opportunities for both customers and businesses.**

## Architecture

The application uses a TypeScript-based full-stack architecture:

```text
React + TypeScript
        ↓
   Vite / Frontend
        ↓
Node.js + Express
        ↓
   Backend Services
```

Using TypeScript across the application provides a consistent language across the frontend and backend while allowing the product to evolve from an initial prototype toward a more structured application.

---

## Product Thinking

Rather than starting with a large feature set, I focused on validating the core marketplace interaction:

**Provider has availability → availability becomes visible → customer discovers it → customer books**

This kept the initial product focused on the fundamental problem: connecting unused appointment capacity with customers who have immediate demand.
